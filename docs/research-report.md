# Build the CRM around money locked with PSUs

S. Prince Hightech's CRM should treat its job as **getting cash released**, not storing records. In PSU industrial-services contracting, almost every business problem ends in one of three outcomes: a bid gets rejected on a technicality, a monthly bill gets held because one compliance document is missing, or money sits with the client (EMD, performance guarantee, retention, TDS, GST-TDS, unpaid wage-revision claims) and nobody chases it. Payment terms make this concrete. A typical PSU service contract pays "within 30 days from the date of submission of bills in proper form & certification by the Engineer-in-charge", and payment is "withheld" until wage, PF, ESI and insurance proofs are attached ([POWERGRID](https://apps.powergrid.in/pgciltenders/Files/0961c741-6404-4684-85c6-6c0da4af6445/TERMS_AND_PROCEDURES_OF_PAYMENT.pdf)). Every missing document therefore restarts the clock, while the contractor keeps paying 5,000+ workers. The market has no product for this. Indian tender tools (TenderTiger, Tender247, BidAssist) stop at award. Construction ERPs (Highrise, StrategicERP, Xpedeon) and site apps (Onsite, Powerplay, RDash) lack PSU-grade statutory payroll. Manpower ERPs (FaME, Ramco) have no tender pipeline. Contract-labour systems (NTPC CLIMS, emsphere, TeamLease RegTech) are built for the principal employer, not the contractor. The planned MVP has the right skeleton: tender register, convert to project, subcontractors, payroll with PF/ESI, GST invoices, DPRs, approvals and dashboard. To be production-ready it needs five things it currently lacks. The first is a fuller tender state machine with document validity gating. The second is a ledger of financial instruments (EMD, PBG, security deposit, retention). The third is a monthly "bill readiness" compliance pack tied to each work order. The fourth is a versioned rules engine for wages, statutory rates and tax. The fifth is a deduction-level receivables reconciliation. Several of these rules changed between late 2025 and late 2026: the Labour Codes, the EPF wage ceiling, GST 2.0, the TDS section and the TReDS mandate. Any hard-coded logic is therefore already out of date.

## A PSU contractor runs three businesses with one cash pool

From the outside, S. Prince looks like a services company. Inside, it runs three linked operations, and each has its own specialists, deadlines and failure modes.

The **tendering business** is staffed by a tender executive or bid manager, an estimator, a documentation and liaison person, and the director who holds the Class 3 DSC. They watch several client portals. NTPC publishes on ntpctender.ntpc.co.in and takes bids on eprocurentpc.nic.in ([NTPC NIT](https://ntpctender.ntpc.co.in/uploads/job_35005.html)). Mahagenco runs an SRM system that needs registration and physical verification before documents can even be downloaded ([Mahagenco NIT](https://mahagenco.in/tenderpdf/Revised%20NIT-4_20250818195418352.pdf)). GeM and CPPP cover central buyers. The team keeps vendor enlistments alive, such as NTPC's dedicated enlistment for "Stone/Boulder and Foreign Material Picking/Removal from Running Belt Conveyor", which requires CHP experience within five years and three supporting POs ([NTPC enlistment](https://ntpctender.ntpc.co.in/Uploads/job_44342.pdf)). They build manpower rate analyses and BOQ prices, arrange EMD instruments, answer clarifications, sit through reverse auctions and negotiations, and convert an LoA into a signed agreement and a performance guarantee.

The **execution and workforce business** is staffed by site in-charges, shift supervisors, safety officers, an HR and labour-compliance team, and payroll staff. They onboard workers through police verification, medical fitness, safety induction and gate passes. They staff 24x7 CHP shifts against contracted deployment numbers. They pay wages within statutory deadlines, deposit PF and ESI, keep statutory registers, handle accidents and unions, and record joint measurements and hindrances with the client's Engineer-in-Charge (EIC). At NTPC, the client's own system (CLIMS) generates the wage sheet from gate biometrics and blocks entry until safety training and medical clearance are recorded ([NTPC CLIMS](https://www.ntpc.co.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/CLIMS-write-up.pdf)). The contractor's records must therefore match the client's, not merely exist.

The **billing and finance business** is staffed by billing engineers, accounts and tax staff, and the director. They raise monthly or running-account (RA) bills, assemble the compliance pack, track each bill through the PSU's portal stages, reconcile the net receipt against a stack of deductions (income-tax TDS, GST-TDS, BOCW cess, retention, penalties), file GST from three registrations, manage bank guarantee limits, and fund payroll for the months before the client pays.

These three operations share one cash pool. A tender priced without the Labour Code cost increases becomes a loss-making contract. A missing ESI challan holds a bill and forces the company to draw more on its cash-credit limit. A BG limit used up by unreturned EMDs stops the company from bidding on the next tender. The CRM's value lies in connecting these flows, and that is where the existing product categories fail.

## Tendering: sixty-odd ways to lose a bid before price is opened

Most tender losses come from process failures before price competition starts. The rules that govern these failures are both strict and changeable.

### Discovery, corrigenda and portal fragmentation

Each client has its own portal, registration process, DSC mapping and corrigendum page, so the first risk is a tender that is missed or seen late. Third-party aggregators exist because firms cannot watch every portal themselves ([tender18](https://tender18.com/tender-bidding/national-thermal-power-corporation-ntpc)). Corrigenda are frequent and run in sequence. POWERGRID's "GeM-Bidding-Corr-8116372-5" is the fifth corrigendum on one bid ([POWERGRID](https://apps.powergrid.in/pgciltenders/Files/1c37dcf9-b89b-42a6-9120-9f220b268c04/66d1f2f1-a19e-4e30-a8ca-45b2508c4091/GeM-Bidding-Corr-8116372-5.pdf)), and an ICMR bid reached its 33rd ([ICMR](https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Tender/1785146405_gem-bidding-corr-9495650-33.pdf)). A single Mahagenco tender carried **five separate deadlines**: query cut-off, pre-bid meeting, online submission, physical submission of the original bid security, and techno-commercial opening. The price-bid opening was to be "intimated by email" ([Mahagenco NIT](https://mahagenco.in/tenderpdf/Revised%20NIT-4_20250818195418352.pdf)). A tender register with one "due date" field cannot represent this.

### Document validity is a rejection engine

E-tenders need a Class 3 organisation DSC. A bid that is unsigned or wrongly signed "is treated as not submitted at all" ([certificate.digital](https://www.certificate.digital/articles/26032616/digital-signature-in-government-tenders-a-complete-guide/)). Practitioners list PAN/GST/Udyam data mismatches and expired ISO or MSME certificates as instant-rejection triggers ([echai.ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/selling-to-government/how-do-i-write-a-tender-bid-that-doesn-t-get-thrown-out-on-a-technicality)). The courts have made it impossible to fix substance after the fact. In a 2025 Delhi High Court case, a CA certificate that put sales under the wrong head failed the criterion, and fresh documents filed afterwards under a different head were rightly refused, because clarifications cover only minor irregularities ([Delhi HC](https://delhihighcourt.nic.in/app/showFileJudgment/59406052025CW20102025_152001.pdf)). For the CRM, this means validity must be checked against each tender's **bid due date and bid validity end**, not against today's date. Experience must also be stored in the exact form the PQ wording requires: executed value, completion date, the plant, and the entity that did the work.

### Bid security, MSE rights and the LoA chain

Under GFR Rule 170, bid security "should ordinarily range between two percent to five percent". It can be a DD, FDR, banker's cheque, BG or online payment, and it must stay valid **45 days beyond bid validity**. MSEs are exempt ([GFR amendment](https://dgll.nic.in/sites/default/files/2023-06/GFR%20amendment2.pdf)). Insurance surety bonds (February 2022) and e-BGs (August 2022) were added as accepted forms ([gconnect ISB](https://www.gconnect.in/orders-in-brief/conduct-rules/amendment-general-financial-rules-gfr-2017-insurance-surety-bonds.html); [gconnect e-BG](https://www.gconnect.in/orders-in-brief/conduct-rules/amendment-general-financial-rules-gfr-2017-acceptance-electronic-bank-guarantee.html)). The MSE exemption has a trap: it covers goods and services, but "Works Contract are excluded", and it never covers the performance guarantee ([Cochin Shipyard FAQ](https://cochinshipyard.in/uploads/MSME/FAQsPPPForMSEs.pdf)). That matters directly for S. Prince. Stone-picking manpower is a service. Painting, cast basalt, structural and civil work may be classed as works, so each tender's classification must drive the EMD decision. On price, MSEs within L1+15% may match L1 for up to **25%** of the tender ([Cochin Shipyard FAQ](https://cochinshipyard.in/uploads/MSME/FAQsPPPForMSEs.pdf)). One law-firm source still says 20%, which looks out of date. The Supreme Court's *Lifecare* ruling (February 2025) held the MSE policy binding ([Fox Mandal](https://foxmandal.in/promise-vs-practice-in-mse-procurement/)). Integrity Pacts apply above organisation-set thresholds. At THDC these are works of Rs. 100 crore or more and goods/services of Rs. 1 crore or more, and the signed IP must be uploaded with the technical bid "or face rejection" ([THDC](https://thdc.co.in/sites/default/files/notice/IntegrityPact2025.pdf)). The COVID-era cut in performance security to 3% was time-bound ([DPE OM](https://dpe.gov.in/sites/default/files/DPE_OM_DTD_19_11_2020_DOE.pdf)). No source shows it extended past March 2023, so the CRM should read the percentage from each NIT rather than assume a value.

### Manpower pricing now has to absorb Labour Code costs

Manpower price bids usually require a wage break-up of basic + VDA, PF, ESI, bonus and service charge ([Canara Bank GeM annexure](https://canarabank.bank.in/media/BREAK%20UP%20OF%20PRICE%20BID%20%20%20GeM%20BID%20ANNEXURE%20191222.pdf)). Since the Labour Codes took effect on 21 November 2025, the uniform wage definition changes PF, gratuity and bonus bases, and fixed-term employees earn gratuity after one year ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)). The single most important commercial fact for manpower bids is that some NTPC contracts are explicitly "on firm price basis throughout the contract period. No escalation". NTPC refused reimbursement even when a bidder raised abnormal minimum-wage hikes ([NTPC cor_20614](https://ntpctender.ntpc.co.in/Uploads/cor_20614.html)). On such a contract, every April and October VDA revision comes straight out of margin unless the bid priced it in.

## Execution: the workforce is the compliance surface

The four Labour Codes took effect on 21 November 2025 and repealed 29 laws, including the CLRA Act ([iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/labour-codes-2026-implementation-complete-guide-to-what-is-in-force-what-changed-and-what-to-do-now/); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)). One blog claims they "remain unenacted". It conflicts with EY, KPMG and others and should be disregarded. The OSH (Central) Rules 2026, notified in May 2026, rebuilt contract-labour licensing ([Sansa Legal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/osh-code-central-rules-2026-notified-by-ministry-on-may-9)). A single licence now lasts **five years and can cover several states**. It is applied for on Shram Suvidha, and renewal is due 90 to 30 days before expiry. **Every contract work order must be reported electronically within 15 days**. Wages must be paid by bank transfer before the 7th day after the wage period ends. If the contractor fails to pay, the principal employer pays within 15 days and recovers the amount from the contractor ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1072788)). The licence threshold rose to 50 workers. Appointment letters are mandatory, overtime is paid at twice the wage rate, and construction-type workers aged 40 and over need an annual medical examination ([Sansa Legal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/osh-code-central-rules-2026-notified-by-ministry-on-may-9)). State rules are uneven. Maharashtra and Delhi had notified rules for all four Codes by May 2026 ([iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/labour-codes-2026-implementation-complete-guide-to-what-is-in-force-what-changed-and-what-to-do-now/)), but no source confirmed Chhattisgarh's status. This matters because NTPC and DVC sites fall in the central sphere, while CSPGCL and MSPGCL sites fall under state labour departments.

Wages and social security changed again in 2026. Central-sphere minimum wages are revised through VDA on **1 April and 1 October**. The April 2026 order put construction unskilled wages at Rs 556–694 a day depending on area ([KS&K](https://ksandk.com/md/newsletter/vda-minimum-wages-2026-india/)), and an order dated 30 September 2026 took effect from 1 October ([DCP Sol](https://www.dcpsol.com/central-sphere-minimum-wages-vda-revised-october-2026)). Chhattisgarh revised its own rates from 1 April 2026 ([WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/work/minimum-wage/updates/2026/minimum-wage-increased-in-chhattisgarh-india-from-01-april-2026-may-09-2026)). The **EPF wage ceiling rose from Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 effective 17 September 2026**, which lifts the maximum employer contribution from Rs 1,800 to Rs 3,000 a month ([Fox Mandal](https://foxmandal.in/news/epfo-wage-ceiling-raised-what-employers-need-to-do-now/)). New EPF, EPS and EDLI Schemes were gazetted in June 2026 ([Moneylife](https://mas360.moneylife.in/article/epfo-overhaul-2026-three-new-schemes-a-20-day-settlement-clock-and-greater-flexibility-on-contributions/4892.html)). ESIC stays at a Rs 21,000 ceiling with contributions of 3.25% (employer) and 0.75% (employee) ([IndPayroll](https://www.indpayroll.com/blog/esic-new-rules-2026-salary-limit-eligibility-contribution-rates-payroll-compliance-guide)). That source gives the ESIC due date as the 21st, against the usual 15th, so the date should be confirmed before it is encoded. Under the "50% wage rule", allowances above half of remuneration count as wages for PF, gratuity and bonus ([iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/labour-codes-2026-implementation-complete-guide-to-what-is-in-force-what-changed-and-what-to-do-now/)). Full and final settlement is due within **two working days** of separation ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)). Every one of these is a parameter with an effective date, which is why a payroll module with fixed rates will produce wrong pay.

The physical risks are severe. At Kota STPS in March 2025, a contract worker cleaning the CHP fell onto a running conveyor, was dragged across several belts and died. The union alleged understaffing on a belt about 1 km long and demanded compensation and a job for a family member ([ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!state/thermal-plant-contractual-worker-dies-after-getting-trapped-in-conveyor-belt-in-kota-enn25030203601)). Similar deaths have been reported at KPCL's RTPS ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Frtps-contract-worker-crushed-to-death-776620.html)) and at NLC ([ETV Bharat](https://etvbharat.com/english/state/tamil-nadu/worker-dies-in-an-accident-on-a-conveyor-belt-at-nlc/na20201117191317720)). Labour pressure in Korba is constant. In April 2026, contract workers blocked a power-plant gate over minimum wages and hours ([WSWS](https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2026/04/11/frpd-a11.html)). At Adani Godda, land-loser workers were moved between outsourcing firms on backdated contracts ([Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1082001/why-workers-at-an-adani-power-plant-who-gave-up-land-for-jobs-went-on-a-hunger-strike)). This explains why the CRM must handle workforce takeovers, worker-origin quotas and dispute registers, and not only attendance.

## Billing and finance: every deduction is a separate reconciliation

A PSU bill does not count as "submitted" until the client accepts the whole compliance pack. NTPC's vendor bill-tracking system shows how this works. The vendor registers the bill online and receives a diary number. It must still send a physical copy, and the bill then moves through "Bill received", "Acknowledged by I/C", "Pending at Vendor"/"In Review", "Accepted", "Parked" and "Posted" on a SAP back end ([NTPC Vendor Manual](https://vendor.ntpc.co.in/public/helpdoc/Vendor%20Manual_bill%20tracking_final.pdf)). POWERGRID's terms list the documents behind that gate. They are an EIC-certified joint measurement certificate, the GST invoice, the deployment list, a wage disbursement sheet, EPF challans, ESI challans with forms, and a workmen's compensation policy, with "payment withheld until submitted". The terms also state **no interest on delayed payment** ([POWERGRID](https://apps.powergrid.in/pgciltenders/Files/0961c741-6404-4684-85c6-6c0da4af6445/TERMS_AND_PROCEDURES_OF_PAYMENT.pdf)). One port authority went further. It demanded bank proof that wages were credited before the 7th and imposed a **15% penalty** for non-compliance ([VOC Port](https://intranet.vocport.gov.in/Circulars/Cir%2001.12.2022.PDF)). The contractor's cash cycle follows from these rules. Wages for month M go out within about seven days and statutory deposits follow. The bill can be submitted only after the challans exist, and payment comes 30 days or more after certification. This is an inference built from the cited timelines, but it means S. Prince is likely financing around two to three months of payroll, plus the GST it pays before the client pays it.

The net receipt then reaches the bank already reduced by several deductions. Each one reconciles to a different government record:

- **Income-tax TDS.** From 1 April 2026 it sits under **Section 393(1) of the Income-tax Act 2025**, replacing s.194C. Companies are deducted at 2%, and supply of labour counts as contract work ([TDSMAN](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/06/tds-on-payments-to-contractors-section-3931-section-194c/)).
- **GST-TDS.** 2% on taxable value for contracts above Rs 2.5 lakh. The deductor files it in GSTR-7 by the 10th, and the credit appears only once the contractor accepts it ([Disytax](https://disytax.com/?p=24194)).
- **BOCW cess.** 1% of construction cost, notified on 8 May 2026 under the Social Security Code and deducted from public-sector works bills ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/analysis-bocw-cess-under-code-on-social-security/)). Pure supply contracts are exempt ([KS&K](https://ksandk.com/newsletter/pure-supply-contract-is-statutorily-exempt-from-levy-under-the-bocw-act/)), and its application to painting and manpower bills is contestable.
- **Retention, penalties, LD and recoveries.** These sit on top of the statutory deductions.

GST itself moved under the contractor's feet. Since **22 September 2025, all works contracts to government entities are taxed at 18%**, and the old 12% concessional slab is gone ([TaxGarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/gst-on-works-contract-construction-services-india-2026)). A contract priced inclusive of 12% before that date now carries a 6-point tax gap that only a change-in-law claim can recover. Manpower supply is taxed at 18% under forward charge for a body-corporate supplier ([TaxGarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/gst-on-manpower-supply-staffing-services-rate-rcm-india-2026)). A 2025 appellate ruling held that a state PSU is not "Government" for the pure-services exemption ([TaxHeal](https://www.taxheal.com/?p=138708)). Procedure has also become unforgiving:

- Businesses with turnover of Rs 10 crore or more cannot report an e-invoice older than **30 days** ([GSTGyaan](https://gstgyaan.com/advisory-time-limit-for-reporting-e-invoice-on-the-irp-portal-lowering-of-threshold-to-aato-10-cr-and-above)). A contractor that dates its invoice and then waits for EIC certification can lose the ability to generate an IRN.
- GSTR-3B is hard-locked to GSTR-1, and a three-year time bar now applies ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-return-filing-rule-changes-from-july-2025)).
- Under Rule 37A, input tax credit must be reversed by 30 November if a subcontractor has not filed GSTR-3B by 30 September ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/decoding-rule-37a-cas-perspective-indias-toughest-itc-provision.html)).

Delayed payment remedies strengthened in 2026, but only for some clients. Micro and small Udyam holders are entitled to payment within 45 days of acceptance and to compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate ([TaxGuru 43B(h)](https://www.taxguru.in/income-tax/section-43bh-msme-payment.html)). The MSMED Amendment Bill passed both Houses in August 2026. It brings 90-day mediation and arbitration, jurisdiction based on the supplier's address, and a 75% pre-deposit on buyer appeals. Its commencement date is unconfirmed ([Vinod Kothari](https://vinodkothari.com/2026/07/strengthening-msme-ecosystem-msmed-amendment-bill/)). Since **30 June 2026, CPSEs must settle MSME invoices through TReDS** ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/government-mandates-treds-for-msme-invoice-settlement-by-cpses)). That covers NTPC and DVC, but the inference is that it does not cover state gencos such as CSPGCL and MSPGCL. Client type therefore decides which remedy is available, and the CRM needs a CPSE / state PSU flag on every client. One caveat follows from the earlier point that the MSE EMD exemption excludes works: S. Prince's Udyam category (micro, small or medium) decides whether any of these rights apply, and the notes do not record that category.

Three conclusions come out of this scan.

**Tender discovery is commoditised, and so is tender-side AI.** BidEasy gives away bidder counts and L1 discounts across 110+ portals ([Infralens](https://tenders.infralens.in/)). TenderTiger sells AI "PQ Checker" and "Pre Bid Query Writer" agents, plus an MCP server ([PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/tendertiger)). Tender247 markets bid/no-bid engines, annexure generators and an "L1 Tracker" ([Tender247](https://www.tender247.com/)). S. Prince should import from these services rather than rebuild discovery. The only crossover claim found was Cur8r's early-stage "post-award project fulfilment support" ([F4 Fund](https://f4.fund/startups/cur8r)).

**The PSU's systems define the contractor's work.** NTPC CLIMS auto-generates wage sheets from gate biometrics, and contractor, EIC, Finance and HR all review them ([NTPC CLIMS](https://www.ntpc.co.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/CLIMS-write-up.pdf)). An EPFO directive requires principal employers to verify PF remittance before paying contractors, and allows them to deduct dues from contractor bills ([Simpliance](https://www.simpliance.in/India/LEI/govt_notification/Central/additional-compliance-for-principal-employer-with-reference-to-employees-engaged-by-or-through-contractors-81)). CLMS vendors advertise "attendance-linked contractor invoicing to eliminate ghost worker billing" ([emsphere](https://www.emsphere.com/?p=29476)). The contractor therefore needs a tool that produces exactly what these systems check, and that reconciles with them before the EIC does. No contractor-side product of this kind was found. That absence is a moderate-confidence finding: regional vendors may exist.

**User complaints reveal the design bar.** For Indian site apps, the complaints are web/mobile inconsistency, no delete or correction in attendance, weak visualisation ([Capterra Powerplay](https://www.capterra.com/p/268357/Powerplay/)), weak search ([Capterra RDash](https://www.capterra.com/p/10010044/RDash/)) and limited workflow customisation ([Software Advice Onsite](https://www.softwareadvice.com/construction/onsite-profile/)). For HRMS, greytHR's location attendance "often times hangs, or does not pick up the location", and payroll support is slow ([Capterra greytHR](https://www.capterra.com/p/150850/greytHR/reviews/)). HROne itself admits that office-first tools "struggle with shop-floor or field realities" ([HROne](https://hrone.cloud/?p=53938)). Procore users cite 10–14% renewal hikes and update churn ([Constructable](https://constructable.ai/blog/procore-reviews-pricing-alternatives)).

Build-versus-buy economics favour building the core. At list prices, running 5,000 workers through greytHR, Zoho or RazorpayX would cost roughly **Rs 28–90 lakh a year**. This is a consultant estimate from the published per-employee rates ([greytHR](https://www.greythr.com/pricing-calculator/); [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/pricing/); [RazorpayX](https://razorpay.com/payroll/pricing)), and those products still would not produce PSU packs. The commodity rails are cheap by comparison. Bulk payouts cost Rs 4–9 per IMPS/UPI and Rs 5 per NEFT, with account validation included ([RazorpayX Payouts](https://razorpay.com/x/payouts_x/)). Specialist offline face attendance costs about Rs 18–19 lakh a year at list price for 5,000 heads ([Truein](https://truein.com/pricing)). The sensible split is to build the worker master, multi-rate wage engine, advances, gate-log reconciliation, bill-pack generator and financial-instrument ledger in-house, and to integrate payouts, optional face attendance, Tally, and a regulatory-update feed. The main risk of building is keeping up with statutory change. The mitigation is architectural: store rules as data, with effective dates and source documents.

## What makes this CRM unique: the contract as the unit of truth

The differentiation does not come from having more modules. It comes from making the **work order (contract)** the object that every other record hangs off. Tenders become contracts. Workers, attendance, payroll, measurements, bills, deductions, instruments and subcontractor bills all point to a contract. The director then sees margin and locked cash per contract, per plant and per PSU. Ramco's case study with a large facilities firm names the payoff directly: closing "the disconnect between operations, billing and payroll" to "reduce the revenue leakages" ([Ramco](https://www.ramco.com/resources/case-study/erp/smooth-run-for-a-top-facility-management-company-with-ramco-erp)).

Eight features would make the product unique in this market:

1. **Tender to Contract conversion that inherits everything.** The manpower schedule, BOQ, EMD and PBG terms, LD and penalty clauses, billing cycle, escalation clause type, tax basis and PSU contacts carry over automatically into the contract, its budget and its billing calendar.
2. **A "cash locked with PSUs" ledger** as a first-class object. It covers EMD, PBG, security deposit, retention, withheld amounts, uncredited GST-TDS and TDS, and pending variation claims, each with a release trigger and a chasing workflow.
3. **A contractor-side bill-readiness pack** per work order per month: wage register, muster, ECR summary and TRRN, ESIC challan, per-worker bank UTR and credit date, and licence and insurance validity. It is formatted per PSU (NTPC, MSPGCL, CSPGCL) and shows "bill blocked until X".
4. **Gate-log reconciliation** of CLIMS or PSU gate exports against supervisor attendance and payroll before submission.
5. **A versioned rules engine** covering minimum wage and VDA by jurisdiction, zone and skill; PF and ESI ceilings and rates; GST rates; TDS sections and codes; EMD and PBG percentages; MSE rules; and IP thresholds. Each rule carries an effective-from date and a source document, so a 1 October VDA order or a 17 September PF ceiling change is a data entry, not a code release.
6. **Bid intelligence from S. Prince's own cost history.** Public L1 data (often free) is combined with actual cost per man-month and per job from payroll and DPRs, to produce a minimum safe bid and expected margin. Tender vendors cannot see this data, and no Indian product doing this for service or O&M contracts was found. Tender247's "Margin Analyst" sits inside its competitor-intelligence suite, which is built on past tender results rather than a bidder's own costs ([Tender247](https://www.tender247.com/)).
7. **Field-first operation.** This means offline supervisor-assisted gang attendance (one device, many workers), DPRs from voice notes and photos in the style of Procore's Daily Log Agent ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251015796723/en/Procore-Advances-the-Future-of-Construction-with-New-AI-Innovations-at-Groundbreak-2025)), Hindi and Marathi interfaces, and WhatsApp payslips.
8. **No-code flexibility.** Custom fields, configurable stages, per-contract checklists and approval chains, with unlimited site and subcontractor users. This answers the "limited customisation" complaint common across the category.

AI belongs on top of this foundation, not in place of it. Commodity AI (tender summary, deadline and PQ extraction) should be matched or integrated. The defensible AI uses internal data. Examples are predicting which bills will be held for missing proofs, checking whether deployable workers and supervisors exist for a tender's manpower schedule, and a director copilot that answers questions like "which NTPC contracts are under 8% margin this quarter?"

## Conclusion

Seen as a CRM, S. Prince's product competes with mature tender tools and HRMS suites and loses. Seen as the contractor-side counterpart to the PSU's own systems, it has almost no direct competitor. Its job is to produce, before the EIC asks, exactly what CLIMS, the bill-tracking desk and the PSU's finance team check, and to keep score of every rupee those checks withhold. The rule changes of 2025–26 deepen that gap rather than close it: the Labour Codes and OSH rules, the EPF ceiling, GST 2.0, Section 393, the BOCW cess notification, the TReDS mandate and the MSMED amendment. Generic products update slowly for the Indian contract-labour corner case, while a contractor that stores rules as dated data can update in a day. The biggest design risk is not missing features. It is building the MVP's modules as separate silos with hard-coded rates, which would reproduce the operations–billing–payroll disconnect the tool is meant to remove.

Some gaps need first-hand confirmation from S. Prince's own staff before the logic is finalised. They are the actual GCC text for JMR, hindrance, retention and escalation at NTPC, CSPGCL and MSPGCL; whether the state gencos run CLIMS-like portals; S. Prince's Udyam category; the current performance-security percentages; Chhattisgarh's Labour Code rule status; and the ESIC due date. The cheapest high-value step now is to take three live contracts (one NTPC manpower, one CSPGCL manpower, one painting or basalt works job) and walk each through the P0 checklist end to end. Wherever the app cannot answer "why is this bill not paid yet, and how much of our money is with this client?", that is the next thing to build.
